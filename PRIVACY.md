# Packet Lens privacy policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

Packet Lens does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- Capture files you open are processed only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
- The app makes no network requests: no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no account.
- Files you save stay in the app's Documents folder on your iPhone (visible in the Files app) until you delete them or remove the app.
- Preferences (language, appearance) are stored locally on the device.

If you share a capture file from the app, it goes only where you send it, through the standard iOS share sheet.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/heyobi/packetlens/issues
